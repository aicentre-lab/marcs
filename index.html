MARCS: Mass Attendance and Realtime Classroom System

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026 · Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MARCS: Mass Attendance and Realtime Classroom System mobile application (the “App”, package name pro.myrollcall.srhu_attendance) accesses, collects, uses, shares, stores and deletes your information. The App is published by Ved TRUST Foundation (“we”, “us”, “our”) for use by students and faculty of Swami Rama Himalayan University (“SRHU” or the “University”). It was developed with the Center for Artificial Intelligence and Research (CAIR), SRHU.

In short
  • The App is only for SRHU students and faculty. The University creates accounts; you cannot sign up in the App.
  • To mark attendance, the App uses your camera (a selfie and a blink check), your precise location and Bluetooth (to detect the classroom beacon). It uses them only while you have the App open for attendance, never in the background.
  • One selfie per attendance is uploaded and compared with your enrolled face photo to confirm it is you.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, use analytics or tracking SDKs, or read device identifiers such as IMEI or advertising ID.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time. See Account and data deletion.

1. Who we are

Ved TRUST Foundation publishes the App on Google Play and is responsible for this Privacy Policy. The App provides attendance services for Swami Rama Himalayan University. The University decides who receives an account and uses attendance records for its academic administration.

For any privacy question or request, contact us at vtrustf@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information (provided by the University)

  • Name, and optionally title and first name
  • University email address
  • Role (student or faculty) and institution
  • An internal user ID
  • Class, course and enrolment details
  • For students, an enrolled reference face photo used for identity checks

You sign in with your email address and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password in readable form.

2.2 Attendance information (students)

When you mark attendance for a class session, the App records:

  • The session, class, course and lecture, and the faculty member running it
  • The date and time, and whether you were marked present
  • Results of each verification step: session PIN, time window, Bluetooth classroom beacon, location and face check
  • Precise location: your latitude and longitude and their accuracy, your distance from the classroom, and whether the location appeared to be faked (mock location). If a location could not be obtained, no coordinates are stored.
  • Attendance selfie: one photo, reduced to at most 1080 pixels wide
  • Liveness result: whether the blink / anti-spoofing check passed, with a score and the number of attempts
  • Face match result: whether the selfie matched your enrolled photo, and a match score

2.3 Classroom presence information (students)

While the in-class screen is open during a session, the App checks from time to time that you are still in the classroom. It records:

  • Whether the classroom Bluetooth beacon was detected, when it was first and last seen, and for how long
  • Whether your location was within the classroom range
  • Basic device state, to help explain verification failures: platform (Android or iOS), whether Bluetooth and location services are turned on, and whether location permission is granted

These checks stop when you leave that screen or close the App.

2.4 Session information (faculty)

When a faculty member starts a session, the App records their name and ID, the session PIN, the time window, the classroom beacon identifiers, and the location of the classroom (taken from the faculty member’s device) that students are checked against.

2.5 Manual attendance (faculty)

Faculty may mark a student present by hand for a short time after a session ends. The App then records who marked the attendance, the reason they gave, and a photo of the student taken by the faculty member. That photo is face-checked in the same way as a selfie.

2.6 Information stored only on your device

  • Your light or dark theme choice
  • A temporary copy of your selfie, kept in private App storage until it is uploaded
  • Your sign-in session and a local cache of App data, kept by Firebase so the App works reliably

2.7 Information we do not collect

The App does not access or collect:

  • Contacts, SMS, call logs, calendar, files, health or financial data
  • Microphone audio
  • Persistent device identifiers such as IMEI, IMSI, SIM serial number or Android ID
  • The Android Advertising ID or App Set ID
  • Your device’s own Bluetooth address
  • Your location in the background

The App contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

4. Face and liveness data

Because face images are sensitive personal data, we explain their handling separately:

  • On your device: the live camera preview is analysed on your phone to find your face, check image quality and confirm you are a live person (blink check and an anti-spoofing model). These camera frames are not uploaded or stored.
  • Uploaded: only the one accepted selfie is uploaded to secure cloud storage.
  • Face matching: a face-verification server operated for the University (rollcall.srhu.edu.in) compares your selfie with your enrolled reference photo. It returns a match result and score, which are saved with your attendance record.
  • Purpose only: face images and results are used only to confirm that the person marking attendance is the enrolled student and to prevent proxy attendance. They are not used for advertising or profiling, or for any other purpose. They are not sold.

On-device face detection uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone. Google may collect limited, non-personal diagnostic and usage information about ML Kit itself, as described in Google’s ML Kit terms. Images are not sent to Google for face detection.

5. How we use information

We use your information only to provide and protect the attendance service:

  • Signing you in and showing you your classes and sessions
  • Verifying and recording attendance using the PIN, time window, classroom beacon, location and face checks
  • Preventing fraud, such as proxy attendance, fake GPS locations and photos of photos
  • Letting faculty run sessions, see the live class roster and review or correct attendance
  • Keeping attendance records for the University’s academic administration
  • Diagnosing problems with attendance verification
  • Meeting legal obligations

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell it.

6. Who can see your information and who we share it with

  • Your faculty: faculty for your classes can see your name, enrolled face photo, attendance status and verification results (including location and face-check outcomes), and live classroom-presence status during a session.
  • Swami Rama Himalayan University: authorised University staff can access attendance records for academic administration.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf, under confidentiality and security obligations:
    • Google Firebase (Google LLC): authentication, database and file storage
    • The face-verification server operated for the University, which receives the attendance record ID, your name and a sign-in token, and reads the two photos needed for the comparison
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, a court order or a valid request from a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety or property of users, the University or others.
  • Organisational change: if the App is transferred to another organisation, information may be transferred with it. We will notify users in advance.

We never publish your information or sell it to anyone.

7. How we protect your information

  • All data sent between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. The App refuses to contact the face-verification server over an unencrypted connection.
  • Data is stored in Google Firebase, which encrypts data at rest.
  • Access is controlled by sign-in and server-side security rules. Students can only read their own records. Faculty can only access data needed to run and review their sessions.
  • Face-verification requests must include a valid, short-lived sign-in token.
  • Production builds of the App do not write personal data, such as location, to device logs.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users and authorities of a data breach as required by law.

9. Account and data deletion

You can ask for your MARCS: Mass Attendance and Realtime Classroom System account and the associated data to be deleted at any time. You do not need to uninstall the App first.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to vtrustf@gmail.com from the email address registered with your App account.
  2. Use the subject line “MARCS: Mass Attendance and Realtime Classroom System – Account deletion request”.
  3. Include your full name and your student or employee ID.

We will confirm your request, verify that it comes from the account holder, and complete the deletion within 30 days. We will email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your login account and profile (name, email, role)
  • Your enrolled reference face photo
  • All your attendance selfies and manual-attendance photos
  • The location coordinates, liveness results, face-match results and device-state information in your attendance and presence records

What may be kept, and why

Swami Rama Himalayan University may need to keep a basic record of your attendance (course, session date and present/absent status) as part of your official academic record, as required by University regulations and applicable law. Any such record will not include photos, face data or location data. It is kept only for as long as the University is required to keep academic records.

Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before it is permanently overwritten.

Deleting your account does not change your enrolment status with the University. If you are still enrolled and your course requires attendance to be marked through the App, please speak to your department before requesting deletion.

10. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Ask us to delete your data (see section 9)
  • Withdraw consent to optional processing. You can revoke camera, location or Bluetooth permission in your device settings at any time, but attendance cannot then be verified through the App.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity
  • Raise a complaint with us, and if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any of these rights, email vtrustf@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
