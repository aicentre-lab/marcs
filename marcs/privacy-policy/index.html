Privacy Policy

MARCS: Mass Attendance and Realtime Classroom System · Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains how the MARCS app (the “App”), published by Ved TRUST Foundation (“we”, “us”), collects, uses and protects your information. The App lets students and faculty of participating educational institutions mark and manage class attendance.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details (provided by your institution): name, email address, role (student or faculty), class and course details, and an enrolled reference face photo for students. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see them.
  • Attendance data: session, date and time, attendance status, and the result of each verification check.
  • Precise location: taken when you mark attendance, to confirm you are in the classroom and to detect fake GPS.
  • Photos: one selfie per attendance, used for face verification. If a faculty member marks attendance manually, they take a photo of the student instead.
  • Bluetooth beacon data: whether the classroom beacon was detected while the attendance screen was open.

We do not collect contacts, messages, files, microphone audio, advertising ID or device identifiers such as IMEI. The App contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: to take the attendance selfie and run the blink (liveness) check.
  • Location: to check that you are within classroom range. On Android, it is also required for Bluetooth scanning.
  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): to detect the classroom beacon.

These permissions are used only while the App is open during attendance, never in the background. You can turn them off in your device settings, but you will then be unable to mark attendance.

3. How we use your information

Only to verify and record attendance, to prevent proxy or fake attendance, and to let faculty and your institution manage attendance records. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Face detection and the liveness check run on your device using Google ML Kit, so camera frames are not uploaded. Only the final selfie is uploaded, where it is compared with your enrolled photo.

4. Sharing

  • Your faculty and institution can see your attendance records and verification results.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (sign-in, database, storage) and a secure face-verification server operated for your institution.
  • Legal requirements: we share information when the law requires it.

5. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored in Google Firebase, which encrypts data at rest. Access is restricted: students can see only their own records, and faculty can see only their own sessions.

6. Data retention

  • Attendance selfies are deleted after 180 days.
  • Account data and attendance records are kept while you are enrolled or employed, and for 1 year after you leave.

7. Account and data deletion

To delete your account and data, email vtrustf@gmail.com from your registered email address. Use the subject “MARCS – Account deletion request” and include your full name and ID.

We will delete your account, photos, location data and verification results within 30 days. Your institution may keep a basic attendance record (course, date, present or absent) where its academic rules require it. That record contains no photos or location data.

8. Children

The App is meant for students and staff of educational institutions and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
